ARCHITECTURE & ORCHESTRATION BRIEF

The missing layer in agentic AI: neural decision engines.

As enterprises move from chat copilots to autonomous multi-step workflows, a structural bottleneck has emerged: routing repeatable judgment through 9–30 second generative reasoning models multiplies latency and token spend. A new class of System One decision models—Cloudflare’s Clef, TypeSafe AI’s Jev, and Google’s Decisions API—turns classification and policy gating into a sub-second, zero-output-token software primitive.

CASE IN POINT · LIVE EXHIBIT

Food by Clef as a single decision peg.

Why a playful food-classification lab is a clean blueprint for production enterprise routing.

On the surface, Food by Clef settles a lighthearted debate—whether a hot dog is a taco or lasagna is a cake. Under the hood, it is a minimal, transparent exhibit of a single-agent decision node operating on Ollama’s /v1/systemone and Cloudflare Workers AI.

Rather than prompting a chat model to write a paragraph and parsing brittle JSON out of prose, the application submits unstructured multimodal evidence—a dish name, a photograph, or both—alongside a typed schema of three simultaneous questions: one nine-way categorical classifier (choice) and two independent binary probability gates (noul) for food presence and structural ambiguity.

In one forward pass, Clef returns calibrated preference scores across all nine categories plus both guardrail probabilities. Change a single structural detail—an open falafel wrap versus a sealed burrito—and the probability mass shifts deterministically from Sushi to Calzone. That exact single-peg behavior is the building block of large-scale orchestrated systems.

SECTION II · ORCHESTRATION TOPOLOGIES

From standalone Plinko to hybrid orchestration.

How orchestrators like Paperclip and LangGraph pair fast decision routers with frontier reasoning agents.

01 · THE MULTI-STEP COST MULTIPLIER

Why reasoning agents stall on repeatable triage

Research from McKinsey and QuantumBlack on enterprise agentic builds highlights a recurring failure mode: unmanaged agent loops multiply token spend and cumulative latency.2 When a frontier reasoning model is invoked at every intake step, simple routing tasks inherit 9–30 second waits and unpredictable variance.

02 · STANDALONE PLINKO CASCADES

Repeatable sorting without generative LLMs

For high-volume, well-defined decisions, teams chain multiple Clef, Jev, or Gemini Decision nodes directly together. Each peg narrows the state—validity gate, domain bucket, priority tier—producing auditable, repeatable outputs at a fraction of generative compute.

03 · HYBRID ORCHESTRATION

Fast router ahead of deep synthesis

Inside orchestrators such as Paperclip (coordinating specialized AI workers) or LangGraph (stateful conditional edges), a decision engine sits at the front door. Clear cases trigger instant deterministic actions; only when an ambiguous noul rises or top-two class probabilities narrow does the orchestrator wake a heavy reasoning agent—handing it pre-scored context.
