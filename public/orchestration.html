THE EXHIBIT & CODEBASE

A playful lab for a serious pattern.

Food by Clef is a fun-loving exhibit built to make a neural decision engine visible. Settling whether a hot dog is a taco or lasagna is a cake is lighthearted—but the exact same single-pass pattern powers high-speed triage, policy checks, and agent routing in production.

The entire decision call lives in a single, readable script—no chat prompt chains, no regex parsing, just a typed schema of one choice rubric and two noul gates.

WORKFLOW DESIGN

Where the model fits.

Input

Define the labels and the checks.

Give each category a distinct description. Food by Clef uses one nine-way choice question for starch shape and two noul questions for food presence and ambiguity.

Decision

Make the threshold explicit.

The classifier flags inputs below 50% food presence. It marks a ruling as uncertain when ambiguity exceeds 50% or the top category scores below 60%. Tune thresholds against labeled examples from your own task.

Orchestration

Give the hard cases a second look.

A workflow can chain small decisions or branch to a person or reasoning agent for synthesis. Pass along the evidence and scores so the next step has the context it needs.
