CASE FILES

Your rulings.

How Clef makes a ruling +

Read the evidence

A name, a description, or a photo gives Clef evidence about the dish. An open wrap and a sealed burrito have different structures.

Score nine shapes

Clef Flash scores the nine Cube Rule categories and checks whether food is present and the evidence is ambiguous.

Inspect the ruling

Compare category scores and review the winning definition. Scores express model preferences. Case history stays in this browser.
